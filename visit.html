Can I drive through?

Yes. Java Ray’s offers drive-through service and takeout. Pull up to the stand at 5520 E Franklin Road, order at the window, and take your drink with you.

Is there something besides coffee?

Absolutely. The menu includes chai tea, hot chocolate, hot or iced tea, flavored steamers, fruit smoothies, Italian sodas, and bottled water. Explore the menu.

Are you open on weekends?

Our listed hours are Monday–Friday, 6 AM–noon. Saturday and Sunday are closed. Call (208) 713-2248 for questions about a specific day.

Is parking accessible?

Wheelchair-accessible parking is available at the stand.

Can I get my coffee delivered?

Java Ray’s serves customers at the stand with drive-through and takeout. Delivery is not listed as an available service.
